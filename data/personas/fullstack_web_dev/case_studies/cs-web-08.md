# A mobile ordering web app for a small business in hospitality

## Context

In 2023 a small business in hospitality asked me to let guests order and pay from a phone at the table.

## What I did

I used Firebase for the central part of the solution. Card payments go through a hosted payment provider, and webhooks confirm every charge. It works comfortably on small screens. The team already knew React, so I used it. I picked Tailwind CSS because it kept the code short and testable.

## Result

Outcome: took over 40% of orders away from the phone line.

## Looking back

Weekly demos kept the client close to the work and removed surprises.
