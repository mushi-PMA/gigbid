# Case study: Telehealth appointment app

## Context

A mid-size company in the healthcare sector came to me in 2025. They needed to let patients book video visits and share forms before the call.

## What I did

Everything starts with a single compose command on any machine. PostgreSQL did most of the heavy lifting. I picked Node.js because it kept the code short and testable. Storage is S3 and compute is EC2 behind a load balancer. React did most of the heavy lifting.

## Result

Outcome: reduced no-shows by 19%.

## Looking back

Handover notes and a recorded walkthrough made the last week calm.
