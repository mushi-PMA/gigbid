# Case study: Used camera gear marketplace

## Context

In 2024 a startup in e-commerce asked me to connect people who sell used camera gear with buyers and hold the money until delivery.

## What I did

- Most of the work was done with Stripe.
- Most of the work was done with TypeScript.
- I used Next.js for the central part of the solution.
- A relational database with migrations and indexes handles the busy queries.
- Hot lookups are served from an in-memory key-value store with expiry.

## Result

Result: reached 1,200 listings in the first quarter.

## Looking back

Most of the delay came from waiting for access, so I now ask for it in week one.
