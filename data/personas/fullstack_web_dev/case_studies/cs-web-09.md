# Insurance customer portal

## Context

A enterprise in the insurance sector came to me in 2025. They needed to give policy holders a login to view documents, make claims and update details.

## What I did

React did most of the heavy lifting. PostgreSQL was the main tool here. Documented HTTP endpoints serve the front end. The team already knew Node.js, so I used it. Every merge runs the tests and builds a release automatically.

## Result

What changed: moved 35% of service calls to self-service.

## Looking back

I kept a short decision log, which saved a long meeting when priorities changed.
