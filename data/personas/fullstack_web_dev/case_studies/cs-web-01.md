# A class-booking platform for studios for a small business in wellness

## Context

In 2025 a small business in wellness asked me to let members book classes, buy passes and join waitlists without phoning the studio.

## What I did

- React did most of the heavy lifting.
- Checkout sessions and webhook handling cover payments, refunds and failed charges.
- Deployments use a pinned build recipe so library versions never drift.
- I used PostgreSQL for the central part of the solution.
- The team already knew Node.js, so I used it.

## Result

Outcome: online bookings grew threefold in two months.

## Looking back

I kept a short decision log, which saved a long meeting when priorities changed.
