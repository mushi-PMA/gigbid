# A subscription storefront for a small business in e-commerce

## Context

In 2024 a small business in e-commerce asked me to sell monthly boxes with pause, skip and swap options customers can manage themselves.

## What I did

- The team already knew Shopify, so I used it.
- Layouts adapt from phone to wide desktop; small screens came first.
- JavaScript did most of the heavy lifting.
- Most of the work was done with Stripe.

## Result

Result: lowered support emails about billing by 45%.

## Looking back

Weekly demos kept the client close to the work and removed surprises.
