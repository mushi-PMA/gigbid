# Case study: Event ticketing site

## Context

A small business in the events sector came to me in 2024. They needed to sell tickets for a festival without overselling when the doors of the sale open.

## What I did

- Hot lookups are served from an in-memory key-value store with expiry.
- Most of the work was done with Stripe.
- The team already knew PostgreSQL, so I used it.
- The service ships as one image that runs the same on a laptop and in the cloud.
- The team already knew Next.js, so I used it.

## Result

What changed: sold 9,000 tickets with no double bookings.

## Looking back

Handover notes and a recorded walkthrough made the last week calm.
