# Case study: Performance and accessibility overhaul

## Context

A mid-size company in the media sector came to me in 2024. They needed to fix a heavy, hard-to-use news front end that failed accessibility checks.

## What I did

I hand-tuned the browser side: bundle size, rendering and keyboard use. It works comfortably on small screens. The team already knew JavaScript, so I used it. Most of the work was done with CSS. The team already knew React, so I used it.

## Result

Outcome: reached a Lighthouse score above 95 and passed the accessibility audit.

## Looking back

Weekly demos kept the client close to the work and removed surprises.
