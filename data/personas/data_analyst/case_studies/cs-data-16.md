# Ad spend reporting

## Context

The client, a small business working in marketing agency, wanted to send clients a clear weekly ad performance report without an analyst copying numbers.

## What I did

Ad spend was joined with orders to see return per channel. I picked SQL because it kept the code short and testable. Looker Studio did most of the heavy lifting. Most of the work was done with Google Sheets. The refresh runs by itself and emails the result.

## Result

Outcome: cut weekly reporting effort per client from three hours to 20 minutes.

## Looking back

Handover notes and a recorded walkthrough made the last week calm.
