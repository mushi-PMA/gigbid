# Case study: Hotel pricing analysis

## Context

The client, a mid-size company working in hospitality, wanted to understand how room rates, seasons and events move occupancy across 12 hotels.

## What I did

- The team already knew Tableau, so I used it.
- Python did most of the heavy lifting.
- Effects were checked with confidence intervals and significance tests.
- I used Pandas for the central part of the solution.
- Seasonality, holidays and trend are handled by week and by day.

## Result

After launch: showed a 9% rate gap that was worth about 300k a year.

## Looking back

Most of the delay came from waiting for access, so I now ask for it in week one.
