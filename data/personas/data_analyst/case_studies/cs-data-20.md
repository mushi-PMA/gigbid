# Case study: Clinical trial data summary

## Context

A enterprise in the healthcare sector came to me in 2023. They needed to summarise outcomes and adverse events from a multi-site study for the review board.

## What I did

- R was the main tool here.
- Duplicates were removed, formats fixed and missing values handled before any analysis.
- Statistics was the main tool here.
- I built the core in Data Visualization.
- A scheduled pack goes to the board with the same layout every period.

## Result

After launch: delivered the review pack two weeks before the deadline.

## Looking back

Most of the delay came from waiting for access, so I now ask for it in week one.
