# A marketing attribution analysis for a scale-up in e-commerce

## Context

In 2024 a scale-up in e-commerce asked me to find out which channels really bring buyers, not just last clicks.

## What I did

Google Analytics did most of the heavy lifting. I picked Looker Studio because it kept the code short and testable. Duplicates were removed, formats fixed and missing values handled before any analysis. SQL did most of the heavy lifting. Ad spend was joined with orders to see return per channel.

## Result

Outcome: moved 18% of ad budget to channels with twice the return.

## Looking back

I kept a short decision log, which saved a long meeting when priorities changed.
