# A supply chain KPI dashboard for a mid-size company in logistics

## Context

In 2024 a mid-size company in logistics asked me to track on-time delivery, stock cover and supplier lead time without chasing five people.

## What I did

- I used SQL for the central part of the solution.
- A central analytics store sits apart from the live application database.
- A governed reporting layer serves the whole business with agreed definitions.
- I built the core in Tableau.
- I picked Microsoft Excel because it kept the code short and testable.

## Result

After launch: improved on-time delivery tracking from monthly to daily.

## Looking back

Weekly demos kept the client close to the work and removed surprises.
