# A sales forecast for a small business in hospitality

## Context

A small business in the hospitality sector came to me in 2024. They needed to plan staff and purchasing for the week from past sales, weather and holidays.

## What I did

- I used Python for the central part of the solution.
- A model predicts next week's values from past patterns.
- Proper sampling and hypothesis tests replaced eyeballing charts.
- Microsoft Excel was the main tool here.
- I used Time Series Analysis for the central part of the solution.

## Result

Outcome: forecast error stayed under 8% for staffing decisions.

## Looking back

Most of the delay came from waiting for access, so I now ask for it in week one.
