# Case study: Employee attrition analysis

## Context

A mid-size company in the hr tech sector came to me in 2023. They needed to find what is driving people to leave within their first year.

## What I did

- I picked Python because it kept the code short and testable.
- The team already knew Microsoft Power BI, so I used it.
- I built the core in Statistics.
- Inconsistent records were tidied first, with a log of every change.

## Result

After launch: identified two manager groups with double the average exit rate.

## Looking back

A small first release with real users taught us more than the planning did.
