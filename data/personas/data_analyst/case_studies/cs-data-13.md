# A competitor price tracker for a small business in retail

## Context

The client, a small business working in retail, wanted to watch 800 competitor products daily and flag price changes.

## What I did

Most of the work was done with Microsoft Excel. A scheduled script replaces the manual steps and runs unattended every morning. Duplicates were removed, formats fixed and missing values handled before any analysis. I built the core in Data Scraping. I used Python for the central part of the solution.

## Result

What changed: helped reprice 150 products within a day of competitor moves.

## Looking back

Most of the delay came from waiting for access, so I now ask for it in week one.
