# Case study: Hospital waiting-time study

## Context

The client, a enterprise working in healthcare, wanted to find out why patients in the emergency department wait so long at certain hours.

## What I did

- I used R for the central part of the solution.
- Inconsistent records were tidied first, with a log of every change.
- I built the core in SQL.
- Most of the work was done with Tableau.
- Proper sampling and hypothesis tests replaced eyeballing charts.

## Result

After launch: identified a shift gap that explained 40% of long waits.

## Looking back

Weekly demos kept the client close to the work and removed surprises.
