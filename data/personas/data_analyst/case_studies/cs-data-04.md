# Month-end reporting automation

## Context

In 2023 a mid-size company in finance asked me to stop the finance team rebuilding the same month-end pack by hand every month.

## What I did

- I picked Python because it kept the code short and testable.
- Extract, reshape and load steps feed the reporting tables each night.
- Most of the work was done with SQL.
- I built the core in Microsoft Excel.
- The refresh runs by itself and emails the result.

## Result

After launch: cut month-end reporting from five days to one.

## Looking back

I kept a short decision log, which saved a long meeting when priorities changed.
