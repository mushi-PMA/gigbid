# Energy usage reporting

## Context

The client, a enterprise working in utilities, wanted to show facility managers hourly energy use against expected use and flag waste.

## What I did

- Seasonality, holidays and trend are handled by week and by day.
- I used Python for the central part of the solution.
- I built the core in SQL.
- I built the core in Microsoft Power BI.
- Extract, reshape and load steps feed the reporting tables each night.

## Result

After launch: pointed to savings of about 7% on the largest site.

I kept a short decision log, which saved a long meeting when priorities changed.
