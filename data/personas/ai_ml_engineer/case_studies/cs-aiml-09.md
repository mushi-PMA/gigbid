# A review sentiment tracker for a small business in hospitality

## Context

Brief (2023, hospitality): read thousands of hotel reviews every month and show managers what guests complain about.

## What I did

- I used Natural Language Processing for the central part of the solution.
- I built the core in Hugging Face.
- A scheduled collector fetches pages politely, respecting rate limits and robots rules.
- Managers see trend charts instead of tables.
- Python did most of the heavy lifting.

## Result

What changed: gave managers a weekly issue list that replaced hours of reading.

## Looking back

A small first release with real users taught us more than the planning did.
