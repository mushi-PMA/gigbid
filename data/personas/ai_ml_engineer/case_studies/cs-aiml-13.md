# Crop disease detection

## Context

A startup in the agritech sector came to me in 2023. They needed to tell farmers from a phone photo of a leaf which disease they are looking at.

## What I did

- TensorFlow was the main tool here.
- Computer Vision did most of the heavy lifting.
- Lighting correction and resizing steps sit in front of the model.
- The model was shrunk to run on a phone and works offline.

## Result

Outcome: reached 91% accuracy on field photos taken in poor light.

Handover notes and a recorded walkthrough made the last week calm.
