# Insurance claim document sorting

## Context

A enterprise in the insurance sector came to me in 2025. They needed to sort incoming claim packets into forms, photos, invoices and letters before an adjuster opens them.

## What I did

Every model version is tracked, tested on a frozen set and promoted by a script. Deep Learning was the main tool here. Python was the main tool here. TensorFlow did most of the heavy lifting. Scanned pages were first turned into lines of text with positions before any model saw them.

## Result

Result: automated sorting of 82% of packets with no manual touch.
