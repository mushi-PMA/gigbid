# A churn prediction model for a mid-size company in media

## Context

In 2023 a mid-size company in media asked me to find subscribers likely to cancel next month so the retention team can call them first.

## What I did

Effects were checked with confidence intervals and significance tests. The team already knew scikit-learn, so I used it. Most of the work was done with Predictive Modeling. Most of the work was done with Python. Tabular preparation was done in dataframes, with checks after every merge.

## Result

What changed: found 61% of churners in the top decile of scores.

## Looking back

Weekly demos kept the client close to the work and removed surprises.
